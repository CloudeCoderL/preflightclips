              Privacy and security

              • Your clips, captions and briefs stay on your device. They are never uploaded.
              • No account, no password, no e-mail. No cookies, no ads, no tracking.
              • The page comes over an encrypted connection and runs only its own code, plus one pinned library for subtitles.
              • The first time you use subtitles, the browser downloads the speech library from jsDelivr and the speech model from Hugging Face. They see a download request, never your clip.
              • If you buy a plan, the server keeps a hash of your key, the public keys of your devices and the id of your payment. No name, no e-mail, no card, no IP address.
              • Nobody from PreflightClips will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is a thief.
