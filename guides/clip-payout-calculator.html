How clipping payouts work

Most clipping campaigns pay a fixed rate per 1,000 views, often between $1 and $3 depending on the platform. The brief also sets a minimum payout: a clip that earns less than that pays nothing, and a maximum payout per post.

So the number to watch is not the total views but the views needed to cross the minimum. At $2 per 1,000 views and a $5 minimum, a clip needs 2,500 views before it pays anything.
