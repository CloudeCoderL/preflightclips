TikTok safe zone for 1080×1920 video

Where TikTok draws its buttons and caption on top of a vertical video, in pixels, and a free overlay template to check your own edit.

On a 1080×1920 video, keep titles, subtitles and logos inside the green frame: about 160 px from the top, 440 px from the bottom and 150 px from the right edge. Everything in red is covered by the app for most viewers.

Why TikTok text gets hidden most often

TikTok shows the longest captions of the three apps, and the caption grows upward when it wraps. Subtitles placed in the lower third of the frame, where most editors put them by default, end up under the caption and the sound name.

One frame for TikTok, Reels and Shorts

If the same clip goes to all three apps, use the strictest margins of each: 250 px top, 460 px bottom, 160 px on each side. The all-apps guide has a combined template.
