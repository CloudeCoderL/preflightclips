Posting

  • Say "no real money" or "play-money tokens" once, in the video or in the caption. Required.
  • Real human voice, or no voiceover at all. AI and text-to-speech narration are rejected at any view count.
  • Use footage you shot yourself. Do not re-cut the spot, slides, or logos we provided. Those are reference, not your submission.
  • The hook: there is a bet about you right now, your friends can see it, and the app will not show it to you.
  • US audiences only. If most of your viewers are outside the US your submission gets rejected, and we do check.
  • End the video pointed at the viewer's friends, not at the app store. Some version of "tag the one in your group who would do this." Required.
  • The app is not the subject of the joke. Your friends are. The app is just what makes their behavior visible.
  • Film the installed app, not the website. A browser URL bar in frame gets rejected.
