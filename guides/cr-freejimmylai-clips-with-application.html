Posting

  • Build every clip around Sebastien Lai or Claire Lai: their interviews, speeches, press appearances and family photos from the asset folder.
  • Hook in the first 1–3 seconds. Open on Sebastien or Claire speaking, or on a family photo.
  • Fast-paced editing, captions and callouts on screen.
  • Minimum 30 seconds. Use only footage from the provided asset folder.
  • Tone is documentary seriousness, not meme edits. It should feel like something you made because you cared, not like an ad.
  • Jimmy Lai is a father. His children have been separated from him for nearly six years.
  • Show who he is through them: the father they grew up with, why they are proud of him, what they are fighting for.
  • He is a hero of our time, not an elderly victim waiting to be rescued. His children speak about him with pride, not pity. Keep that tone.
  • Jimmy Lai founded Apple Daily, Hong Kong's most widely read pro-democracy newspaper, forced to close in 2021.
  • He was arrested in 2020 under Hong Kong's National Security Law and sentenced to 20 years.
  • He is 78, in poor health, and has spent nearly six years in solitary confinement.
  • He had everything to lose. He spoke when staying quiet would have protected his wealth, comfort and status.
  • Use their words exactly as spoken. Do not splice separate sentences into a new statement. On-screen captions must match the audio.
  • Do not write captions or on-screen text in their voice (e.g. "I just want my dad home"). Quote them in quotation marks and attribute by name.
  • Only feature family members who appear in the asset folder. Do not pull from their personal social accounts.
  • Featuring his children does not mean they endorse your clip. Do not imply they made, approved or shared it.
  • Post must stay live for at least 90 days. Do not edit the caption or post settings after submission.
  • Do not use AI-generated depictions, voices or video of Jimmy Lai or any family member. No AI watermarks.
  • Do not fabricate quotes, court outcomes, or statements.
  • Do not use Jimmy Lai's name or likeness in your username, handle, display name, bio, profile photo or banner.
  • Do not imply you are run by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Jimmy Lai, his family, or the Support Jimmy Lai campaign.
  • All views must be organic. No purchased or bot engagement, no pods, no paid boosting, no re-uploading the same clip across accounts you control.
